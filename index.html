Our Soul & Heritage

We built this space so beauty visits feel like healing, not a chore.

Maison Éthérée started with a simple belief: you shouldn't have to shout over loud music or breathe chemical fumes just to get your hair cut. We wanted a sun-drenched sanctuary smelling of warm rosewood, fresh lavender tea, and quiet acoustic music.

Every chair in our sanctuary is private and spacious. Before we touch a strand of your hair or apply an elixir to your skin, we sit with you over a freshly brewed floral tonic to listen to your morning routine, texture history, and how you want to feel.
